# Define selected-book acquisition policy

## Goal
Avoid downloading every possible book blindly. Use a selected-book policy.

## Tasks
- [ ] Prioritize language lexicons.
- [ ] Prioritize tafsir and hadith commentaries.
- [ ] Prioritize reliable aqidah/manhaj works.
- [ ] Prioritize fiqh/usul books with clear principles and rulings.
- [ ] Prioritize tarajim/tabaqat books with stories and narrator judgments.
- [ ] Avoid abridgements where comprehensive editions exist.

## Acceptance
Every downloaded book has a reason for inclusion.
