# Extract stories, anecdotes, benefits, and admonitions

## Goal
Capture inspirational or explanatory reports that support rich footnotes.

## Tasks
- [ ] Detect story markers.
- [ ] Extract anecdotes from biography/history books.
- [ ] Extract benefits and wisdom statements.
- [ ] Extract admonitions and warnings.
- [ ] Link them to themes and people.

## Acceptance
“Explain further” can retrieve valuable scholarly benefits, not just definitions.
