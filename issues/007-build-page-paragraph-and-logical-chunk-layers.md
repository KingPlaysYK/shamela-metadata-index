# Build page, paragraph, and logical chunk layers

## Goal
Support both precise footnotes and broad research retrieval.

## Tasks
- [ ] Keep page layer.
- [ ] Add paragraph layer.
- [ ] Add logical chunk layer.
- [ ] Allow multiple chunks per page.
- [ ] Avoid chunks that are too small or too large.
- [ ] Preserve overlap and page context.

## Acceptance
Footnotes retrieve focused evidence while research can retrieve broader discussions.
